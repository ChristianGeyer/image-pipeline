Title:
ISSF Technical Rules – Paper Targets and Scoring

Organization:
International Shooting Sport Federation (ISSF)

Year:
2022/2023 Approved Version

Scope:
Defines the official dimensions, tolerances and scoring rules for ISSF paper targets used in 10 m air rifle and 10 m air pistol events.

--------------------------------------------------
SCORING SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------

Rifle and pistol targets may be scored in:
- Full ring values (integer: 10, 9, 8, …)
- Decimal ring values (e.g., 10.0 – 10.9) when electronic or electronic-paper systems are used

Decimal scoring:
Each full scoring ring is subdivided into 10 equal-width radial bands.
Scores are:
10.0, 10.1, …, 10.9
9.0, 9.1, …, 9.9
etc.

This means the radial scoring resolution is:
1/10 of the width of a scoring ring.  :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

--------------------------------------------------
10 m AIR RIFLE TARGET
--------------------------------------------------

Target type: Paper target for 10 m air rifle (standing)

Ring diameters (mm):  :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
10 ring: 0.5 ± 0.1
9 ring: 5.5 ± 0.1
8 ring: 10.5 ± 0.1
7 ring: 15.5 ± 0.1
6 ring: 20.5 ± 0.1
5 ring: 25.5 ± 0.1
4 ring: 30.5 ± 0.1
3 ring: 35.5 ± 0.1
2 ring: 40.5 ± 0.1
1 ring: 45.5 ± 0.1

Special:
- The 10-ring is a white dot (0.5 mm).
- There is no black bullseye at the center.
- Black area extends from ring 4 to ring 9 (diameter 30.5 mm).
- Ring thickness: 0.1 to 0.2 mm.
- Minimum visible target card: 80 mm × 80 mm.
- Background card recommended: 170 mm × 170 mm.

Implication:
The 10-ring diameter is only 0.5 mm → sub-millimeter accuracy is required to score correctly.

--------------------------------------------------
10 m AIR PISTOL TARGET
--------------------------------------------------

Target type: Paper target for 10 m air pistol

Ring diameters (mm):  :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
10 ring: 11.5 ± 0.1
9 ring: 27.5 ± 0.1
8 ring: 43.5 ± 0.2
7 ring: 59.5 ± 0.5
6 ring: 75.5 ± 0.5
5 ring: 91.5 ± 0.5
4 ring: 107.5 ± 0.5
3 ring: 123.5 ± 0.5
2 ring: 139.5 ± 0.5
1 ring: 155.5 ± 0.5

Inner 10:
5.0 ± 0.1 mm

Black area:
From ring 7 to ring 10 = 59.5 mm diameter

Ring thickness:
0.1 to 0.2 mm

Minimum visible target card:
170 mm × 170 mm

Zone numbering:
Rings 1–8 numbered, 9 and 10 are not numbered.

--------------------------------------------------
SCORING PRECISION REQUIREMENT
--------------------------------------------------

For electronic or electronic-paper systems:
- Required accuracy is at least one-half of one decimal scoring ring.  :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

This implies:
If one decimal ring is (ring width / 10), the scoring error must be less than (ring width / 20).

For 10 m air rifle:
Ring width between 10 and 9 rings:
(5.5 mm – 0.5 mm) / 2 = 2.5 mm radial spacing → 0.25 mm per decimal ring.
Half of that ≈ 0.125 mm required accuracy.

Therefore, ISSF scoring requires **sub-0.2 mm localization precision** for top-level competition.

--------------------------------------------------
Relevance to my thesis:

These specifications define the physical limits that any vision-based scoring system must meet. For 10 m air rifle, the 10-ring is only 0.5 mm in diameter, and decimal scoring divides this into 0.1-ring steps, meaning bullet hole centers must be localized with approximately 0.1 mm precision to be ISSF compliant.
